Standalone 2048³ f16 GEMM, K-contiguous weight, strict: cuBLAS 24.6 µs, wgmma n256 34.0 (0.73×), n128 34.9, n64
48.9, best mma.sync 69.3. The wgmma tier is six atoms, five kernel-IR statements, the descriptor builder, the
warp-group drain and one corpus case (`matmul/f16-wgmma-ss-tma-sm90.yaml`), all correct on the H100.

What the day established, in one line each:

- Decode GEMVs are CTA-starved, not bandwidth-bound; the K split fixes that at the price of a second launch.
- Prefill GEMMs on mma.sync stop at the 64×64 tile, one wave; wgmma doubles the throughput where its layout applies.
- An N-contiguous B is correct under wgmma only at a 64-column tile; every wider tile was wrong wholesale.
- The two fused decode attention forms recover with a placement cut; the two prefill forms are compiler bugs.
- Failure rows in the tune DB silently disqualify later pins; the walk benched receipts as targets and stopped at
  the first exception. Both are fixed or worked around in the PR.

## Landed the same night

- **The atom-major B (PR #782).** Every row of the `~/tune/wgmma4/` acceptance is strict-correct: 2048³ `[K, N]` f16
  n64 48.2 µs → n128 32.9 / n256 33.0, cp.async 36.0, the K-contiguous n256 unchanged at 33.4. The corpus expectation
  did NOT hold: at S=512 a 128-wide N tile halves the grid (k_proj 8.4 → 9.7 µs, v_proj 7.7 → 8.8, down_proj 18.6 →
  23.8 at `w4x1`) and a `g2k` / `g4k` split of the down_proj shape lands at 19 µs. The corpus rows stay as recorded.
- **The prefill SDPA + o_proj build failure (PR #783).** The cone seam lowers a twice-read fold once
  (`dedup_recomputes`); the golden-bench target builds at sm_90 and the minimized corpus case
  `attention/f16-sdpa-oproj-residual-computed-a-sm90` guards it. The target has no measured row yet.
- **`make test` on this VM.** Current main fails 25 tests here (`~/tune/wgmma4/failed.txt`, baseline copy
  `~/emmy-main`): 20 strict-evidence errors from serving tests, because the card has no hardware golden, the fp4 cell
  test, and two script tests. Diff a branch's failures against that list; the branch checkouts are `~/emmy-atom` and
  `~/emmy-sdpa`, the quick-run scripts `~/tune/wgmma4*.sh`.

## Next steps, re-ranked after the night's findings

1. **sm_90 hardware golden and node freeze** (was 8). Beyond reproducibility, it is what makes the H100 `make test`
   green (the 20 strict-evidence failures above) and what the prior lacks (its cold pick on the 2048³ GEMM was a
   721 µs scalar tile). `emmy/compiler/pipeline/search/goldens/h100_sm90.yaml` plus freeze rows, via the
   collect-node-data skill from `~/emmy-main`.
2. **The MLP cut's producer child** (was 3). After `PLACE@map.2/inner.1/map=cut` the materialized norm runs as a
   direct kernel (12 µs decode, 92 µs prefill) because a bare `WORK`/`REDUCE` pin reaches both children and an edited
   child receipt did not reach the producer. A child-scoped pin, or a tuner pass over the children, takes decode from
   25 to about 8 µs (Inductor 7.7) and prefill from 143 to about 45. Still the largest corpus payoff.
3. **Launch shape, not instruction width, for the S=512 prefill linears.** The atom-major result says these GEMMs
   are grid-bound at 64-128 CTAs: the wide tile loses 15-30% and a K split does not recover it. What is left is a
   smaller M tile under the wide instruction, or a persistent / stream-K launch; measure a hand row before building.
4. **wgmma attention** (was 2, stage 4 of `plans/hopper-wgmma.md`): Q staged once and read through a descriptor, K and
   V by TMA with the transposed descriptor bit, P in registers as the register-form A operand, the chunked carrier on
   a producer band. The B geometry is settled for both orientations now. Pair it with the exp folding of
   `plans/attention-optimization-memo.md`, which stops being optional on Hopper.
5. **A measured row for the prefill SDPA + o_proj + residual target**, now that it builds: `--record-greedy` against
   a fresh DB on `~/tune/h100-s512.yaml`, then splice. Then the score-statistics form, which still fails
   `lower: no extent for coordinates ['in6']` on every card; compiler work.
6. **Single-launch decode GEMV** (was 4). The atomic split (`g<n>a`) cannot bench without the accumulator-reset
   primitive already recorded for split-K; alternatively the `t32x8` two-dimensional thread block the catalog drops
   today. Worth 15-40% on four decode targets.
7. **Strict tolerance on the fused fp16 residual add.** Every decode down_proj row is one element in 1024 off eager by
   four ulps; the public rounding boundary before the residual add is the same defect seen on V100.
8. **Make the failure-row disqualification visible**, and scope it: a `bench_fail` row for one config should not veto
   a different pinned config of the same kernel.
9. **The corpus oracle for bf16** compares against zeros; the wgmma cases had to be f16.
10. **The paper** can now say what the H100 does: RESULTS.md carries the numbers and their caveats.
