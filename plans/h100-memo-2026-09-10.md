## Summary of the day

| corpus target (Inductor / Emmy, five strict repeats) | decode | prefill |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| input RMSNorm | **1.25** | **2.20** |
| q_proj (+ statistic) | 0.59 | 0.70 |
| k_proj (+ cast) | 0.84 | **1.03** (wgmma) |
| v_proj | 0.83 | 0.99 |
| attention + o_proj + residual | **1.04** (cut route) | no realizable row |
| v_proj + SDPA / softmax × V | 0.75 | 0.12 |
| post-norm + gate/up + SiLU | 0.30 | 0.17 |
| down_proj + residual | 0.02 (strict-failed rows) | 0.63 |
| q/k norm + RoPE + score statistics | Inductor fails | fails to lower |

Standalone 2048³ f16 GEMM, K-contiguous weight, strict: cuBLAS 24.6 µs, wgmma n256 34.0 (0.73×), n128 34.9, n64
48.9, best mma.sync 69.3. The wgmma tier is six atoms, five kernel-IR statements, the descriptor builder, the
warp-group drain and one corpus case (`matmul/f16-wgmma-ss-tma-sm90.yaml`), all correct on the H100.

What the day established, in one line each:

- Decode GEMVs are CTA-starved, not bandwidth-bound; the K split fixes that at the price of a second launch.
- Prefill GEMMs on mma.sync stop at the 64×64 tile, one wave; wgmma doubles the throughput where its layout applies.
- An N-contiguous B is correct under wgmma only at a 64-column tile; every wider tile was wrong wholesale.
- The two fused decode attention forms recover with a placement cut; the two prefill forms are compiler bugs.
- Failure rows in the tune DB silently disqualify later pins; the walk benched receipts as targets and stopped at
  the first exception. Both are fixed or worked around in the PR.

## Future work, ranked by expected payoff over effort

1. **Per-atom TMA deposit for a wide N-contiguous B.** One `(64 K × 64 N)` box per swizzle atom, landing at
   `atom · 64 · 128` bytes, so the descriptor's leading offset is that block stride and the K core-group stride stays
   1024. Frees `n128` / `n256` on `[K, N]` weights, which is every linear of the corpus: q_proj, v_proj and down_proj
   should move from 0.6-1.0× to at or above Inductor. Touches `TmaTransport.fill` and the drain's descriptor geometry;
   the isolation runs in `~/tune/wgmma2/` are the acceptance (every row strict-correct).
2. **wgmma attention** (stage 4 of `plans/hopper-wgmma.md`): Q staged once and read through a descriptor, K and V by
   TMA with the transposed descriptor bit, P in registers as the register-form A operand, the chunked carrier on a
   producer band. Pair it with the exp folding of `plans/attention-optimization-memo.md`, which stops being optional on
   Hopper. Target: the prefill softmax × V and score-statistics forms, once (5) unblocks them.
3. **The MLP cut's producer child.** After `PLACE@map.2/inner.1/map=cut` the materialized norm runs as a direct kernel
   (12 µs decode, 92 µs prefill) because a bare `WORK`/`REDUCE` pin reaches both children and an edited child receipt
   did not reach the producer. A child-scoped pin, or a tuner pass over the children, takes decode from 25 to about
   8 µs (Inductor 7.7) and prefill from 143 to about 45.
4. **Single-launch decode GEMV.** The atomic split (`g<n>a`) cannot bench without the accumulator-reset primitive
   already recorded for split-K; alternatively the `t32x8` two-dimensional thread block the catalog drops today.
   Worth 15-40% on four decode targets.
5. **The two prefill attention bugs.** SDPA + o_proj emits the online-softmax state twice inside the o_proj's
   computed-A fill (duplicate `acc*__c0` declarations); the score-statistics form fails `lower: no extent for
   coordinates ['in6']`. Both also fail or hang on A100, 4090 and 5090; both are compiler work, not tuning.
6. **Strict tolerance on the fused fp16 residual add.** Every decode down_proj row is one element in 1024 off eager by
   four ulps; the public rounding boundary before the residual add is the same defect seen on V100.
7. **Make the failure-row disqualification visible**, and scope it: a `bench_fail` row for one config should not veto
   a different pinned config of the same kernel.
8. **sm_90 hardware golden and node freeze.** The prior has no Hopper evidence: the cold pick on the 2048³ GEMM was a
   scalar tile at 721 µs. `emmy/compiler/pipeline/search/goldens/h100_sm90.yaml` plus freeze rows are what makes an
   H100 number reproducible off this VM.
9. **The corpus oracle for bf16** compares against zeros; the wgmma case had to be f16.
10. **The paper** can now say what the H100 does: RESULTS.md carries the numbers and their caveats.
